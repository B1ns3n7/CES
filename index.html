Login-Logout Monitoring System

By accomplishing this form, you fully give consent to the collection and use of data that you will be providing. You are aware that the information shall be collected for the purpose of the community extension report of NU Laguna. The said data will not be used for any other purposes. All of this will be done in accordance with Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Please read the following instructions carefully:

  • This system tracks your community service activities.
  • All fields are mandatory and must be filled out accurately.
  • You will need to provide:
    • Community Location
    • Project Name
    • Your Full Name
    • Your Email Address
  • When you click "Login", the system will:
    • Record your login time
    • Record your location
  • When you click "Logout", the system will:
    • Record your logout time
    • Record your location
    • Calculate total time spent
    • Display a summary of your service
  • While logged in, you may upload files. They will be saved to Google Drive and renamed automatically.
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection for accurate tracking.
